# Q11: When the input contains multiple channels or actors, what should determine whose information is eligible for retention?
**Stage:** ingestion · **Sources:** 6 · **Marked load-bearing by:** 1

## Camps

### Semantic retrieval
- **Answer:** Underspecified. Dialogue, multi-agent interaction, and episodic/semantic writes are discussed without a data-subject or consent model
- **Taken by:** coala_inverted_for_kivi (1)
- **Assumes:** the stated privacy and consent boundary is enforceable.

### Policy-dependent handling
- **Answer:** Build a separate trace/card for each participant; facts about another person are retained in that person’s own trace, even if mentioned during someone else’s topic
- **Taken by:** creem-decision-inversion-for-kivi (1), tracemem-inverted-for-kivi (1)
- **Assumes:** the stated privacy and consent boundary is enforceable.

### Per access scoping pii
- **Answer:** Per-user access scoping and PII redaction are required, but the write eligibility of third-party content is underspecified
- **Taken by:** inverted_decisions_memory_for_autonomous_llm_agents (1)
- **Assumes:** the stated privacy and consent boundary is enforceable.

### Hybrid or layered strategy
- **Answer:** Distills separate “user-specific” experience traces and memory cards for both conversational participants
- **Taken by:** tracemem-inverted-for-kivi(1) (1)
- **Assumes:** the source's described workload, data shape, and operating constraints apply.
